# Demo fixtures

Place only owned, licensed, or explicitly permissioned clips and source tabs in
this folder. For each demo, add an entry to `demo-manifest.json` with the audio
and source paths, rights holder/license, and intentional seeded mismatch
locations. Do not commit commercial recordings or scraped Ultimate Guitar
tabs.

The manifest may point at a locally generated fixture (`generator` instead of
`audio_path`) while the product slice is being demonstrated. Replace that
entry with a permissioned recording before any client or production demo.
